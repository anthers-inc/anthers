# Subscription Tiers

---

Bluebell subscriptions are the main way the platform supports creators. When you subscribe, your monthly payment is split between a **Creator Pool** (distributed automatically based on what you watch and play) and a **Boost Pool** (funds you can direct toward your favorite creators to unlock their premium content).

Every tier also contributes 3% to the **Community Resilience Fund (CRF)**, which covers shared infrastructure, free-tier hosting, and community reserves.

The pattern is simple: every $5 you add goes straight to your Boost Pool, giving you more to direct toward creators you care about.

## Free Tier

The free tier gives you a taste of what Bluebell has to offer. You get **10 hours of content per month** at up to **720p quality** -- enough for a few evenings of exploring the platform.

Free users don't contribute to the Creator Pool, so creators won't earn revenue from your views directly. Think of it as a way to browse and discover before committing. If you share a Bluebell link with a friend, they can start watching right away without hitting a paywall.

As you approach your monthly limit, you'll see a gentle reminder to subscribe -- no aggressive pop-ups, just a heads-up that you're getting close.

## Seedling -- $5/month

The entry-level paid tier. Your subscription covers infrastructure costs and ensures every creator you watch receives a share of your contribution.

- **25 hours/month** of content
- **Full quality** streaming (up to 4K where available)
- **$4.85** goes to the Creator Pool, distributed automatically based on your watch time
- **$0.15** goes to the Community Resilience Fund

At this tier, all of your contribution flows through the automatic watch-time system -- no manual allocation needed. Creators earn roughly **$0.19 per hour** of your viewing time. This is a great option if you want to support the ecosystem without managing individual creator contributions.

The 25-hour cap is generous for casual use (about an hour a day, five days a week), but daily viewers will naturally want to upgrade for unlimited access.

## Sprout -- $10/month

This is the tier the platform is built around. It introduces the **Boost Pool** -- the mechanism that lets you unlock premium content and direct funds to specific creators.

- **Unlimited** watch time
- **Full quality** streaming (up to 4K where available)
- **$4.70** goes to the Creator Pool (automatic, based on watch time)
- **$5.00** goes to your Boost Pool (you choose how to distribute it)
- **$0.30** goes to the Community Resilience Fund

### How the Boost Pool works

Your Boost Pool funds can be distributed in two ways:

1. **Automatically** -- By default, boost funds are split proportionally based on your watch time, just like the Creator Pool.
2. **Manually** -- You can use allocation controls to direct your boost funds to specific creators. Want to give more to your favorite game developer? Slide their allocation up.

When your boost allocation to a given creator crosses their gate threshold, their **premium content unlocks** for you. This might include subscriber-only posts, early access builds, behind-the-scenes content, or other gated material.

With unlimited watch time, you never have to worry about rationing your viewing -- just enjoy the content and let the system handle the rest.

## Blossom -- $15/month

For users who want to support more creators at the premium level. The extra $5 goes entirely to your Boost Pool.

- **Unlimited** watch time
- **Full quality** streaming
- **$4.55** to the Creator Pool
- **$10.00** to your Boost Pool
- **$0.45** to the Community Resilience Fund

With double the boost budget of the Sprout tier, you can unlock premium content from more creators simultaneously or give a larger allocation to fewer creators you're especially invested in.

## Canopy -- $20/month

The highest standard tier, with the largest Boost Pool.

- **Unlimited** watch time
- **Full quality** streaming
- **$4.40** to the Creator Pool
- **$15.00** to your Boost Pool
- **$0.60** to the Community Resilience Fund

## How Your Money Flows

No matter which paid tier you choose, here's what happens to your subscription each month:

1. **3% goes to the CRF** -- This funds shared infrastructure, keeps the free tier running, and builds community reserves.
2. **The Creator Pool portion is distributed automatically** -- Based on your watch time, play time, reading time, and listening time across all creators you engaged with that month. You don't need to do anything.
3. **The Boost Pool portion is yours to direct** -- Either let it distribute automatically like the Creator Pool, or manually allocate it to the creators you want to support most. Your boost allocation determines which premium content you can access.

The Creator Pool stays roughly stable across tiers (around $4.40--$4.85). When you upgrade, the increase goes almost entirely to your Boost Pool. Upgrading isn't about paying more for platform access -- it's about giving yourself more money to direct toward creators you care about.
