# What is Bluebell?

---

Bluebell is a federated, creator-first media platform built on the AT Protocol. Creators host games, videos, audio, and writing -- keeping 100% of their earnings with real infrastructure costs passed through transparently.

## The Problem

Traditional platforms take a significant cut of creator revenue -- often 30% or more -- while offering limited transparency about where that money goes. Creators are locked into walled gardens with opaque algorithms and unpredictable policy changes.

## Bluebell's Approach

Bluebell takes a fundamentally different approach:

- **No percentage cut** -- Creators keep 100% of what they earn. Infrastructure costs (payment processing, storage, bandwidth) are passed through at cost with full transparency.
- **Community Resilience Fund (CRF)** -- A small 3% contribution from transactions funds shared infrastructure, free-tier hosting, and community reserves.
- **Federation via AT Protocol** -- Built on the same protocol as Bluesky, Bluebell supports portable identity and decentralized content ownership.
- **Multi-format content** -- Games, video, audio, and rich text posts all live on one platform.

## What Can You Do on Bluebell?

### As a User
- Discover and play games from independent creators
- Follow creators and get personalized feeds
- Subscribe to support the platform and unlock premium content
- Watch videos, listen to audio, and read posts

### As a Creator
- Publish games with flexible pricing (free, pay-what-you-want, or fixed price)
- Create rich text posts, upload video and audio content
- Set up a creator profile and build a following
- Receive payments directly through Stripe Connect
- Host and participate in game jams
